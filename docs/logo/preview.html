Media Shuttle — SD card as a shuttle bus

The card's chamfered corner does double duty as the windshield rake, and the driver's window repeats the same 45° cut. Three marks share one grid: the full one above 96px, a simplified one from 32 to 64, and a micro one at 16 to 24, where a bus is sixteen pixels wide.
